# Interpretation limits

- This artifact is a publication subset. The downstream six-table scope was selected after original seed1013 results; representative real-error and module-ablation displays are also result-based selections. The included results do not establish universal superiority or a new blind benchmark.
- All prescribed seeds for included conditions are preserved. The full three-seed statistic is unavailable when a prescribed seed is unavailable; CDC/QSAR remains N/A rather than a two-seed mean or fabricated zero.
- LLM+code includes recorded Pure LLM fallbacks. A successful output does not imply independent code generation succeeded for every seed.
- External-school qualification is separate from repair quality. JobPosting is 2/3; module Retinopathy and Airfoil are each 2/3; both digit70 conditions are 2/5 for both datasets; reuse Airfoil is 1/3. Research-prior outputs are not qualified deployment skills.
- CDC is a timed CPU adaptation and GARF uses the recorded SQLite port. Movies/Raha+HoloClean has candidate-domain and case/format round-trip limits. Movies/CDC has high-cardinality category and partially skipped internal model-search paths. Low scores are not claims that all original baseline algorithms are generally ineffective.
- The Fig.4 comparison uses the same 9B weights with its recorded temperature 0.7 sampler. Other main studies use temperature 0; their settings and denominators must not be merged.
- The single-error-type robustness protocol retains its original four-family quota. Its nominal 30% condition is approximately 7.5--7.6% actual single-family pollution, unlike the separate direct 70% digit-replacement study.
- Fig.7 cost reports accumulated model-request time, not comparable total wall-clock latency. The historical six-table cost ledger and new-batch reuse are separate experiments; no cross-hardware overall speedup is claimed.
- File verification and recorded-score recomputation are not full model retraining. A fresh run must independently prepare inputs, learn, validate, freeze, execute and score. Three or five seeds and mean +/- sample SD do not establish statistical significance.
